BootSkills — EdTech Learning Marketplace

Project information

  • Category: Web — EdTech
  • My Role: Co-Founder & Full-Stack Engineer
  • Company: BootSkills
  • Tech Stack: React, Vite, GraphQL API, Supabase, TanStack Query, Stripe, PWA
  • Live URL: bootskills.com

An EdTech learning marketplace designed to make practical, job-ready digital skills more accessible across Africa. Built the platform end to end.

Key features: Course publishing, Learner enrolment, Payments, Scholarships, Certification, Instructor course marketplace.

A two-sided marketplace where instructors upload and sell courses and learners pay flexibly, with a scholarship pathway so cost is not a barrier to quality training. Learners are placed into projects and internships through partners: Inzira Initiative, Bootskills Lab and Community Engaged for Development (CED).
